write out the CDM equations in manuscript-ready form, organized by physical process

  1. Atmospheric Properties Saturation Vapor Pressure (Antoine equation) \[P_{sw}(T) = \exp\left(A_w + \frac{B_w}{T + 273.15 + C_w}\right)\]

where \(A_w = 18.927\), \(B_w = -4169.63\), \(C_w = -33.57\), and \(P_{sw}\) is in mmHg.

Density of Wet Air \[\rho_A = \frac{M_w , P_{sw}(T, RH) + M_a \left(1 - P_{sw}(T, RH)\right)}{82.061 \left(T + 273.15\right)}\]

where \(M_w = 18.015\) g/mol (water), \(M_a = 28.851\) g/mol (air), and:

\[P_{sw}(T, RH) = \frac{1}{760}\exp\left(A_w + \ln\frac{RH}{100} + \frac{B_w}{T + 273.15 + C_w}\right) \quad \text{[atm]}\]

Dynamic Viscosity of Wet Air \[\mu_A = K_0 + K_1 T + K_2 P_{sw}(T, RH)\]

where \(K_0 = 1.765 \times 10^{-4}\), \(K_1 = 4.752 \times 10^{-7}\), \(K_2 = -1.478 \times 10^{-4}\) [g cm\(^{-1}\) s\(^{-1}\)].

Dew Point Temperature \[T_{dp} = \frac{B_w}{\ln\left(P_{sw}(T, RH) \cdot 760\right) - A_w} - 273.15 - C_w\]

Wet Bulb Temperature \(T_{wb}\) is obtained by solving iteratively (TOMS 748 root-finding):

\[P_{sw}(T_{dp}) - P_{sw}(T_{wb}) - P \frac{M_a}{M_w} \frac{C_{pa}(T_{wb}) \left(T_{wb} - T\right)}{\Delta H_v(T_{wb})} = 0\]

where the heat capacity of air is:

\[C_{pa}(T) = \frac{A + BT + CT^2 + DT^3}{M_a} \quad \text{[cal g$^{-1}$ °C$^{-1}$]}\]

with \(A = 6.917\), \(B = 9.911 \times 10^{-4}\), \(C = 7.627 \times 10^{-7}\), \(D = -4.696 \times 10^{-10}\), and the heat of vaporization is:

\[\Delta H_v(T) = \Delta H_0 \left(1 - \frac{T + 273.15}{647.3}\right)^N \quad \text{[cal g$^{-1}$]}\]

with \(\Delta H_0 = 717.22\) and \(N = 0.332\).

Wet Bulb Temperature Depression \[\Delta T_{wb} = T - T_{wb}\]

  1. Wind Velocity Profile Logarithmic Wind Profile \[u(z) = \frac{U_f}{\kappa} \ln\frac{z - h_C}{z_0}\]

where \(\kappa = 0.40\) (von Kármán constant), \(h_C\) is canopy height [m], and \(z_0\) is friction height [m].

Friction Height \[z_0 = 0.00341 + 0.1245 , h_C\]

Friction Velocity (single measurement) \[U_f = \frac{u(z_1) , \kappa}{\ln\left(\frac{z_1 - h_C}{z_0}\right)}\]

Friction Velocity (multiple measurements) \[U_f = \frac{\kappa \sum_{i} u_i}{\sum_{i} \ln(z_i / z_0)}\]

Richardson Number (atmospheric stability) \[Ri = \frac{g}{\overline{T} + 273.15} \cdot \frac{\partial T / \partial z}{\left(\partial u / \partial z\right)^2}\]

where \(\overline{T}\) is the mean temperature between measurement heights.

Horizontal Wind Variation (ψψψ) Entered directly, interpolated from a Richardson number lookup table, or computed via the SDTF empirical relationship:

\[\psi\psi\psi = 0.524 , h_C + 69.398 , |Ri|\]

  1. Droplet Size Distribution Bi-modal Normal CDF Model The cumulative volume fraction is modeled as a mixture of two normal distributions:

\[F(d_p) = w_1 \left[\Phi\left(\frac{d_p - \mu_1}{\sigma_1}\right) - \Phi\left(\frac{d_{p,\min} - \mu_1}{\sigma_1}\right)\right] + (1 - w_1) \left[\Phi\left(\frac{d_p - \mu_2}{\sigma_2}\right) - \Phi\left(\frac{d_{p,\min} - \mu_2}{\sigma_2}\right)\right]\]

where \(\Phi\) is the standard normal CDF, and parameters \(\mu_1, \mu_2, \sigma_1, \sigma_2, w_1\) are fitted by non-linear least squares (Levenberg–Marquardt).

Probability Density Function \[f(d_p) = w_1 , \phi\left(\frac{d_p - \mu_1}{\sigma_1}\right) \frac{1}{\sigma_1} + (1 - w_1) , \phi\left(\frac{d_p - \mu_2}{\sigma_2}\right) \frac{1}{\sigma_2}\]

where \(\phi\) is the standard normal PDF.

  1. Nozzle Velocity Spray Exit Velocity (Bernoulli) \[v_i = \sqrt{\frac{2 P_N}{\rho_L}}\]

where \(P_N\) is nozzle pressure [Pa] and \(\rho_L\) is solution density [kg m\(^{-3}\)].

Streamline Geometry The spray fan (angle \(\theta\)) is discretized into \(N_s = 11\) streamlines. For the \(i\)-th streamline:

\[\alpha = \frac{180° - \theta}{2}, \quad \Delta\theta = \frac{\theta}{N_s}, \quad \theta_i = -\alpha - \frac{\Delta\theta}{2} - i , \Delta\theta\]

Initial Velocity Components \[V_{z,0}^{(i)} = v_i \sin\theta_i, \qquad V_{x,0}^{(i)} = v_i \cos\theta_i\]

  1. Droplet Transport (Governing ODE System) The trajectory of each droplet is described by a system of six coupled ODEs. The state vector is \(\mathbf{y} = [Z, X, V_z, V_x, M_w, V_{vwx}]^\top\):

Kinematic Equations \[\frac{dZ}{dt} = V_z\]

\[\frac{dX}{dt} = V_x\]

Vertical Momentum (gravity + buoyancy + drag + evaporation thrust) \[\frac{dV_z}{dt} = \frac{1}{M_w + M_s}\left[\frac{\pi}{8} C_D(Re_z) , \rho_A , D^2 , (-V_z)|V_z| + V_z , W + V_D , g \left(\rho_A - \frac{M_w + M_s}{V_D}\right)\right]\]

Horizontal Momentum (wind drag + evaporation thrust) \[\frac{dV_x}{dt} = \frac{1}{M_w + M_s}\left[\frac{\pi}{8} C_D(Re_x) , \rho_A , D^2 , (V_{vwx} - V_x)|V_{vwx} - V_x| + V_x , W\right]\]

(set to zero when \(V_{vwx} \leq 0\))

Evaporation \[\frac{dM_w}{dt} = -W\]

Local Wind Velocity \[\frac{dV_{vwx}}{dt} = \begin{cases} V_z \dfrac{U_f}{\kappa(Z - h_C)} & \text{if } Z > z_0 \ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}\]

Auxiliary Quantities Droplet volume: \[V_D = \frac{M_w}{\rho_W} + \frac{M_s}{\rho_S}\]

Droplet diameter: \[D = \left(\frac{6 V_D}{\pi}\right)^{1/3}\]

Reynolds number: \[Re = \frac{\rho_A , D , \sqrt{V_z^2 + (V_{vwx} - V_x)^2}}{\mu_A}\]

Termination Condition Integration stops when \(Z \leq z_0 + h_C\) (droplet reaches canopy/ground).

  1. Drag Coefficient Clift and Gauvin (1970) correlation:

\[C_D(Re) = \frac{24}{Re}\left(1 + 0.197 , Re^{0.63} + 2.6 \times 10^{-4} , Re^{1.38}\right)\]

  1. Evaporation Rate Ranz–Marshall mass transfer correlation:

\[W(M_w, Re) = \frac{3\pi^{2/3}}{2 \cdot 6^{2/3}} , \lambda_w , \Delta T_{wb} , \rho_W \left(\frac{M_s}{\rho_S} + \frac{M_w}{\rho_W}\right)^{1/3} \left(1 + 0.276\sqrt{Re}\right) \frac{M_w}{M_s + M_w}\]

where \(\lambda_w = 76.4 \times 10^{-8}\) cm\(^2\) s\(^{-1}\) °C\(^{-1}\) is the evaporation coefficient.

  1. Terminal Velocity \(V_t\) is solved iteratively from:

\[V_t = \sqrt{\frac{4 , d_p , g , (\rho_L - \rho_A)}{3 , \rho_A , C_D(Re_t)}}\]

where \(Re_t = \rho_A V_t d_p / \mu_A\).

The maximum deposition time is:

\[t_{\max} = \delta \cdot \frac{h_N}{V_t}\]

where \(\delta = 60\) (default scale factor).

  1. Initial Conditions For droplet diameter \(d_p\) on streamline \(i\):

\[Z(0) = h_N - \delta_L, \quad X(0) = 0\]

\[V_z(0) = V_{z,0}^{(i)}, \quad V_x(0) = V_{x,0}^{(i)}\]

\[M_w(0) = \frac{\pi}{6} d_p^3 , \frac{1 - x_{s,0}}{x_{s,0}/\rho_S + (1 - x_{s,0})/\rho_W}\]

\[M_s = \frac{\pi}{6} d_p^3 , \frac{x_{s,0}}{x_{s,0}/\rho_S + (1 - x_{s,0})/\rho_W}\]

\[V_{vwx}(0) = \frac{U_f}{\kappa} \ln\frac{h_N - h_C}{z_0}\]

where \(\delta_L = 0.1016\) m (liquid sheet offset) and \(x_{s,0}\) is the initial mass fraction of dissolved solids.

  1. Deposition Calculation Domain Discretization The field is divided into spray segments (\(N_{sa}\)) and drift segments (\(N_{da}\)):

\[N_{sa} = \frac{F_D}{d_N}, \quad N_{da} = \lambda , N_{sa}\]

with segment widths \(\Delta W_{sa} = F_D / N_{sa}\) and \(\Delta W_{da} = \Delta W_{sa}\).

Segment midpoints:

\[x_j = \begin{cases} \Delta W_{sa}(0.5 + j) & j < N_{sa} \ F_D + \Delta W_{da}(0.5 + j - N_{sa}) & j \geq N_{sa} \end{cases}\]

Spray Volume Distribution \[V_{sprayed} = \frac{IAR \cdot A}{\rho_L \cdot x_{active}}\]

Partial volume for droplet size class \(i\):

\[SVP_i = f(d_{p,i}) , \Delta d_p , \frac{V_{sprayed}}{N_{sa}}\]

where \(\Delta d_p = 0.5\) μm.

Concentration Matrix (plume spreading) For each streamline \(n\), droplet class \(i\), and segment \(j\):

\[CM_{i,j} = \sum_{n=0}^{N_s - 1} \frac{SVP_i / N_s}{\Delta W_j \left(P_L + 2 , x_j \tan(\psi\psi\psi \cdot \zeta)\right)}\]

where \(\zeta = 2.5\) (one-sigma multiplier for directional spread), and the term in parentheses represents the crosswind plume width at distance \(x_j\).

Ground Deposition Profile \[D(x) = \frac{\sum_i CM_{i,\text{seg}(x)}}{IAR / 10000} \times 100 \quad [% \text{ IAR}]\]

Output at intervals \(\Delta x\) from \(0\) to \(L_{\max}\).

  1. Numerical Integration The ODE system is solved using CVODE (SUNDIALS) with:
  • BDF method (order up to 5)
  • 10,000 output steps per droplet trajectory
  • Relative tolerance: \(10^{-4}\)
  • Absolute tolerances: \([10^{-8}, 10^{-8}, 10^{-8}, 10^{-8}, 10^{-10}, 10^{-8}]\) for \([Z, X, V_z, V_x, M_w, V_{vwx}]\)
